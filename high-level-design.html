One Flex contact centre for all your WhatsApp businesses.

Agents work from one Flex desktop. Behind the scenes, each WhatsApp Business Account keeps its own Twilio subaccount, sender and templates.

The idea

Flex sits at the centre. Each WABA connects through its own Twilio subaccount, and a lightweight integration makes sure every message uses the right one.

The account model

One place for agents to work, without combining the WhatsApp accounts behind the scenes.

What stays consistent

Companies can choose different sender rules without changing the overall setup.

Separate WhatsApp accounts

Each WABA keeps its own sender, templates, traffic and account settings.

Different ways to choose a sender

The WABA can come from the queue, customer context, an agent's choice or a mix of these.

One WABA per conversation

Once a conversation starts, its WABA and sender stay the same.

Add WABAs without rebuilding Flex

Each new WABA follows the same pattern and is added through configuration.

How we would deliver it

Start with one WABA, prove the pattern, then add the others.

1
Agree how it should work

Confirm which teams use each WABA, how senders are chosen and when a conversation ends.

2
Build the shared connection

Create the Flex plugin, WABA registry and messaging service that connects the accounts.

3
Connect and test the first WABA

Test inbound messages, agent replies, templates, delivery updates and routing.

4
Add the remaining WABAs

Connect each subaccount, apply its sender rules, test it and roll it out.

Where credentials live

Subaccount credentials stay in the backend. The Flex plugin only sees the WABA and sender information an agent needs.
